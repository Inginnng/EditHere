# EditHere 0.14.1

- **Native Fast Capture & Window Targeting**: Added `edithere-cli windows` and one-shot `capture --output / --annotate` (`--screen`, `--window`, `--window-id`), backed on Windows by occlusion-resistant `PrintWindow(..., PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT)` with GDI blank-frame fallback, self-process exclusion, ambiguity/minimized guards, atomic PNG writes, and unified Qt + B2 unsaved-changes protection.
- **Smart Web Capture Settlement**: Replaced the fixed 5s `networkidle` stall in `capture-web.mjs` with critical-resource idle tracking (`300ms` quiet window capped at `1500ms`, ignoring SSE/WebSocket/HMR streams) plus double-`rAF`, shared `BrowserContext` reuse per `(viewport, dpr, theme)`, and bounded concurrency (`1..6`).
- **Single-Call AI Capture Fast Path**: Added `edithere_capture` and `edithere_windows` to `cli/unified-mcp.mjs` and `connector/mcp-server.mjs` (`0.2.2`), paired with `desktop-locator` probe caching, auto-generated output paths, and a lazy 60s-idle-evicting warm Playwright browser pool.
- **Snappier IPC Startup & Safe Cancellation**: Switched IPC startup polling to progressive `15ms -> 25ms -> 40ms -> 60ms` micro-backoff and ensured interactive capture cancellation exits cleanly without wedging sessions.
