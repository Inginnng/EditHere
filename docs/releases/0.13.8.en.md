0.13.8 fixes the capture interface being invisible over remote desktops.

- When using EditHere through NetEase UU Remote, ToDesk, Parsec, a cast receiver, or a screen recorder, the selection frame, shade, and toolbar are now visible instead of leaving only a frozen desktop.
- Normal captures still freeze the desktop at the moment of the shortcut; scrolling captures keep the selection border out of the stitched pixels through native window holes.
- Capturing directly on the local display is unchanged.
