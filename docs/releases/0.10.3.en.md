This update fixes text recognition for small, narrow captures and the resize cursor on the blue capture selection.

- **Text recognition for small images**: narrow images are enlarged before recognition. The 380×19-pixel URL sample is read in full, and text highlights still match the original image.
- **Capture selection cursor**: hovering within the resize area around a blue selection corner immediately shows the resize arrow. The cursor stays consistent during and after dragging, with no crosshair drawn over it.
- Added regression tests for recognition of the small image, scaling limits and coordinates, and cursor behaviour across display scales and selection modes.
